## Attend and find materials

Lectures meet Tuesday and Thursday, 12:45–2:45 PM, in SEC 1.321. Extension students attend via Zoom. Each class is two hours, normally about 75 minutes of concepts followed by a hands-on tutorial. Recordings are available within 24 hours.

Use [Ed Lessons](https://edstem.org/us/courses/97373/lessons) for lecture materials and PDFs. Ask course-content and technical questions in [Ed Discussion](https://edstem.org/us/courses/97373/discussion). Ed requires course access.

[Supplementary readings](/readings.html) provide additional learning materials. Independent reading is part of the course.

## What the topics connect

Virtual environments and containers make components reproducible. Networking, service accounts, IAM, and storage connect those components to cloud infrastructure. Data labeling and versioning make data work traceable.

The LLM sequence covers RAG, tools and agents, agent workflows, and fine-tuning methods such as LoRA. Training workflows introduce experiment tracking with tools such as Weights & Biases, multi-GPU setups, and Vertex AI jobs. Inference and workflow lessons connect models to services and repeatable pipelines.

The application sequence connects REST APIs and frontends, usability and code organization, automated tests, continuous integration, Kubernetes scaling, infrastructure as code, and continuous delivery. Performance, cost, evaluation, monitoring, and reliability are system-design decisions throughout.

TensorFlow and PyTorch appear in examples. GCP is the primary cloud platform. Equivalent AWS materials exist, but AWS is not demonstrated in lectures and AWS-specific support is limited.

The teaching team develops the Formaggio reference project during the semester. Apply the same concepts to your own team's project; the reference project does not replace your team's independent work.
